## About
Storck is a free public donation platform for food pantries, backed by an inventory ledger that serves both the organizations internal tracking the public needs list.
Organizations can register their organization via a reviewable form and can associate a user as an admin. This user will have control of all organization actions.
Users can login and select items to donate to their desired organization. Once they have selected their desired items to donate, they can proceed to checkout. At checkout, they will confirm the items that they are donating and select a drop off day. After confirming at checkout, a receipt will be generated for both user and organization, and emailed to the respective emails
